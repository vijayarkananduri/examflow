# ExamFlow — Semester Study Planner
ExamFlow is a private, browser-based semester study planner designed to help students organize their entire academic workload in one focused workspace. It transforms syllabi, subjects, units, topics, and exam dates into a practical daily study plan.
Students can import or manually create syllabi, organize topics by subject and unit, build exam timetables, track study sessions, monitor topic progress, and follow personalized daily recommendations. The planner prioritizes topics based on upcoming exams, confidence levels, revision dates, estimated study time, and actual study progress.
ExamFlow also includes spaced-review tracking, study streaks, weekly insights, readiness metrics, timer-based study sessions, timetable planning, and backup/export tools. Study data is stored locally in the browser, making the app private, lightweight, and usable without an account or backend.
